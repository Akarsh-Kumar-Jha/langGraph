Day 1 – Arrival & Settling In

Morning

  • Arrive at Goa Dabolim International Airport.
  • Taxi to the hostel (≈ 1 hr 30 min, ₹350).
  • Check‑in, unpack, freshen up.

Afternoon

  • Light lunch at Honey Restaurant (local Goan thali, ≈ ₹250).
  • Relax on the hostel’s rooftop, enjoy sea view.

Evening

  • Dinner at a nearby beach shack – try prawn balchão (≈ ₹300).
  • Early night to recover from travel.

Day 2 – Panjim City View Point & Miramar Beach

Morning

Afternoon

  • Beachside lunch – fish curry rice (≈ ₹300).

Evening

  • Return to hostel (≈ 1 hr 15 min, ₹300).

Practical Tips for Goa

  • • Carry a reusable water bottle – tap water isn’t safe to drink.
  • • Use sunscreen (SPF 30+) and a hat for beach days.
  • • Cash (₹) is preferred at small shacks; ATMs are common in North Goa.
  • • Respect local culture – dress modestly when away from beaches.
  • • Taxis can be negotiated; agree on price before boarding.
  • • Most attractions are open year‑round; sunrise‑sunset times are ideal for photography.
